# Julie Nutrition website

The website presents Julie's Nutrition and Batchcooking content to visitors.

## Language

**Page**:
An independently editable presentation of either Nutrition or Batchcooking, made
up of ordered Sections. The Homepage is separate from these Pages.
_Avoid_: Global, service page

**Section**:
A piece of Page content with its own presentation, such as testimonials, pricing,
or an overview. Its position determines where it appears within the Page.

**Homepage**:
The site's entry experience, guiding visitors to the Pages for the currently
available offerings.
